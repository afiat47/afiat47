## About me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh, Software Engineer with  professional R&D experience developing production-grade software for Linux-based networking platforms, with hands-on experience in system architecture, feature development, testing, troubleshooting, and cross-team collaboration. Experienced in network technologies, with additional hands-on development experience in Android Studio, Java/Spring Boot, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, Docker, Kubernetes/AKS, Azure, Azure DevOps, and ArgoCD through cloud-native projects. Strong understanding of Linux, distributed systems, CI/CD, and software engineering practices, with a proven ability to design reliable systems, document technical solutions, and collaborate effectively with engineering teams.

- 🔌 **Embedded & networking:** C on VxWorks, RFC-compliant protocol implementations, Arduino/robotics, Verilog
- ☁️ **Backend & cloud:** Spring Boot, Laravel, PostgreSQL/MySQL, Docker, Kubernetes (AKS), GitOps with ArgoCD
- 🧠 **Computer vision & ML:** YOLOv8, TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV, classical image segmentation
- 🔭 **Outside work:** astronomy and rocketry, which is where [SkyScope 3D](https://github.com/afiat47/SkyScope-3D) came from
